Day 1

First contact with key people, mandate and method presented, first governance markers set.

Week 1

Field diagnostic, meetings with the teams, mapping of risks and first value levers.

Week 2

Prioritized roadmap, costed 100-day plan, steering committee set up with a fixed rhythm.

Month 1

PMO up and running, first quick wins under way, value creation plan shared with the investor.

Month 2

Synergies identified and being captured, tighter tracking of gains, monthly investor reporting in routine.

Month 3

Synergies delivered and measured, governance stabilized, handover to the teams or continuation of the mission.

3Stage 3 · One of the value creation levers
Full spend audit

EBITDA from the first months

Every expense scrutinized, line by line: employee health and benefits, car fleet, telecoms, energy, insurance, software, outside services. We renegotiate, cut what is not needed, and lock the savings into the budget.

Map

All spend, by category and by supplier, in three weeks. You finally see where the money goes.

Renegotiate and reduce

Tenders on benefits, fleet, telecoms, energy, insurance and software. And consume less, not just pay less.

Lock in

Savings written into the budget, tracked monthly with the fund and management. They do not leak back.

Delivered: €460 k a year of savings identified on €27 M of purchases, at an industrial company under LBO · HRIS for the NUWARD subsidiary (EDF) deployed at its creation.
